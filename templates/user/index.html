Welcome to Government Polytechnic Aurai
Welcome to Babu Paras Nath Maurya Government Polytechnic Aurai, Ugapur, Bhadohi (GP Aurai). Established in 2010, we are an AICTE-approved institution under the Board of Technical Education Uttar Pradesh (BTEUP). Our college offers three branches: Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, and Electronics Engineering, with admissions through JEECUP. At Babu Paras Nath Maurya Government Polytechnic Aurai , we focus on providing quality technical education supported by experienced faculty and modern facilities. We aim to equip our students with the skills and knowledge needed for their professional growth. Explore our website to learn more about our programs and resources.

Welcome to our college, where education is the foundation of success and lifelong learning. We are committed to providing quality education in a supportive environment that encourages students to achieve academic excellence, develop strong values, and become responsible citizens.
